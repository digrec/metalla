# Precious Metals Domain Glossary

This reference glossary provides domain context for precious metal portfolio tracking in Metalla.

## ⚖️ Units of Measure

- **Troy Ounce (oz t / ozt):** Standard unit for precious metals. $1\text{ ozt} = 31.1034768\text{ grams}$. (Never confuse with standard avoirdupois ounce $= 28.3495\text{ g}$).
- **Gram (g):** Standard metric unit for small bars and fractional coins.
- **Kilogram (kg):** $1\text{ kg} = 1000\text{ g} \approx 32.1507\text{ ozt}$.
- **Tola:** Traditional South Asian unit. $1\text{ tola} = 11.6638\text{ g} \approx 0.375\text{ ozt}$.

## 🥇 Metal Types

- **Gold (Au):** Most common investment metal.
- **Silver (Ag):** High industrial and monetary utility; traded in larger weight volumes.
- **Platinum (Pt):** Rare industrial and jewelry metal.
- **Palladium (Pd):** Heavily utilized in automotive catalytic converters and electronics.

## 🪙 Product Forms

- **Bar / Ingot:** Poured or minted metal blocks, typically with lower premium over spot.
- **Coin:** Government-minted legal tender (e.g., American Gold Eagle, Canadian Maple Leaf, Vienna Philharmonic).
- **Round:** Privately minted coin-shaped metal pieces with no legal tender status.
- **Grain:** Very small fractional measurement ($1\text{ grain} \approx 0.0648\text{ g}$).

## 📊 Market & Financial Terms

- **Spot Price:** Current market price for 1 troy ounce of raw, unminted metal for immediate delivery.
- **Premium:** Amount charged over spot price (covers manufacturing, distribution, dealer margin, and numismatic value).
- **Spread:** Difference between dealer buy price (bid) and sell price (ask).
- **Fineness / Purity:** Proportion of pure precious metal in parts per thousand:
  - `.999` (Three nines fine) $= 99.9\%$ pure.
  - `.9999` (Four nines fine / "Four nines") $= 99.99\%$ pure (e.g., Maple Leaf).
  - `.9167` (22 karat) $= 91.67\%$ gold alloyed with copper/silver for durability (e.g., Krugerrand, Gold Eagle).
